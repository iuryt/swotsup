About

SWOT maps sea surface height in two ~60 km swaths on each side of its track. This is the AVISO/DUACS Level-3 Low Rate SSH, v3.0 Basic product, on a 2 km grid: .

AVISO distributes one NetCDF file per pass. Here the whole mission is a single IceChunk store, cut in chunks of one passage by ~1000 km of track, so a region reads only the chunks that cross it, from anywhere, with icechunk and xarray.
