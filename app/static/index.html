About SWOT's Up

SWOT measures sea surface height in two 60 km swaths on each side of its track. SWOT's Up shows the whole mission, Level-2 KaRIn Low Rate SSH (version D): 2 km everywhere, 250 m when you zoom in ().

  • Click a point for its time series over the whole mission.
  • Draw a line for its profile, or its Hovmöller over the whole mission.
  • Download the data on screen as NetCDF, or copy the Python that gets the same data.
  • Share the link: it reopens exactly this view.

The data. The files, one per passage, are combined into a single cloud dataset (Icechunk, on Arraylake), versioned and readable with xarray. By default the SSHA includes the crossover correction, the DTU25 mean sea surface, an experimental cross-track calibration and an editing flag (rain, sea ice). You can switch each one off in Display.
